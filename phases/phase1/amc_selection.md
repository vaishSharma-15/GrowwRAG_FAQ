# AMC Selection Document

## Overview
This document outlines the Asset Management Company (AMC) selection process and rationale for the Mutual Fund FAQ Assistant project.

## Selection Criteria

### Primary Criteria
1. **Market Presence**: Established AMC with significant AUM (Assets Under Management)
2. **Scheme Diversity**: Wide range of fund categories (equity, debt, hybrid, commodity, index)
3. **Data Availability**: Comprehensive public information on scheme details
4. **Digital Presence**: Active online presence with detailed scheme information

### Secondary Criteria
1. **Reputation**: Strong track record and regulatory compliance
2. **Accessibility**: User-friendly website and documentation
3. **Transparency**: Clear disclosure of scheme information

## Selected AMC: Axis Mutual Fund

### Rationale for Selection

#### Market Position
- Axis Mutual Fund is one of the fastest-growing AMCs in India
- Consistent performance across fund categories
- Strong brand recognition and trust among investors

#### Scheme Diversity
Axis offers a comprehensive portfolio including:
- Large-cap and Flexi-cap funds
- Small-cap funds
- Sectoral and thematic funds
- Index funds
- Debt funds
- Hybrid funds
- Fund of Funds (FoF)
- Commodity funds (Gold, Silver)

#### Data Accessibility
- Well-maintained website with detailed scheme information
- Comprehensive factsheets available
- Clear NAV history and performance data
- Regular updates on scheme information

#### Digital Presence
- User-friendly website interface
- Mobile application for investors
- Active social media presence
- Educational content for investors

## Alternative AMCs Considered

### HDFC Mutual Fund
- **Pros**: Largest AMC in India, extensive scheme portfolio
- **Cons**: Very large corpus might make data extraction complex

### ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund
- **Pros**: Strong market presence, diverse schemes
- **Cons**: Complex website structure

### SBI Mutual Fund
- **Pros**: Government-backed, high trust factor
- **Cons**: Legacy systems, less modern digital presence

### Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund
- **Pros**: Good performance track record
- **Cons**: Less comprehensive online documentation

## Final Decision

**Selected AMC**: Axis Mutual Fund

**Justification**:
- Optimal balance of scheme diversity and data accessibility
- Modern digital infrastructure facilitates data extraction
- Comprehensive documentation available for all schemes
- Strong reputation and regulatory compliance
- Suitable for pilot implementation of the FAQ assistant

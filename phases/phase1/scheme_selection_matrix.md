# Scheme Selection Matrix

## Overview
This document presents the scheme selection matrix for Axis Mutual Fund, detailing the selected schemes across diverse categories for the Mutual Fund FAQ Assistant project.

## Selection Criteria

### Category Diversity Requirements
- Large-cap funds
- Flexi-cap funds
- Small-cap funds
- Index funds
- Commodity funds (Gold/Silver)
- Fund of Funds (FoF)

### Scheme Selection Criteria
1. **Direct Growth Plan**: To ensure consistency and avoid regular plan complications
2. **Category Representation**: At least one scheme from each major category
3. **Data Availability**: Comprehensive information available on Groww
4. **Investor Interest**: Popular schemes with high investor interest
5. **Unique Characteristics**: Distinct features to demonstrate system capabilities

## Detailed Scheme Information

### 1. Axis Silver FoF Direct Growth
- **Category**: Fund of Funds - Commodities
- **Investment Objective**: To generate returns by investing in units of Axis Silver ETF
- **Key Features**:
  - Exposure to silver prices
  - Low minimum investment
  - No need for demat account
- **Data Points Available**:
  - Expense ratio
  - Exit load
  - NAV history
  - Holdings composition
  - Riskometer

### 2. Axis Small Cap Fund Direct Growth
- **Category**: Equity - Small-cap
- **Investment Objective**: To generate long-term capital appreciation by investing predominantly in equity and equity-related instruments of small-cap companies
- **Key Features**:
  - High growth potential
  - Diversified small-cap portfolio
  - Experienced fund management
- **Data Points Available**:
  - Expense ratio
  - Exit load
  - Minimum SIP amount
  - Portfolio composition
  - Performance metrics
  - Riskometer

### 3. Axis Flexi Cap Fund Direct Growth
- **Investment Objective**: To generate long-term capital appreciation from a diversified portfolio of equity and equity-related securities
- **Key Features**:
  - Flexible allocation across market caps
  - Can invest in large, mid, and small-cap stocks
  - Dynamic portfolio management
- **Data Points Available**:
  - Expense ratio
  - Exit load

  - Riskometer

### 4. Axis Gold Fund Direct Growth

## Justification for Selection

### Why These 5 Schemes?
1. **Category Diversity**: Covers 6 different fund categories
2. **Investor Relevance**: Popular schemes with high investor interest
3. **Data Richness**: Comprehensive information available for extraction
4. **Unique Features**: Each scheme has distinct characteristics to test system capabilities
5. **Manageable Scope**: 5 schemes provide adequate variety without overwhelming the pilot phase
